Why not just use Google Alerts?+
Google Alerts misses 40% of relevant signals and 90% of what it delivers is noise. Even when it finds a signal, it gives you a link — no freight context, no HS codes, no contact name, no message written, no LinkedIn sent. FreightSignal takes you from "signal detected" to "LinkedIn connection sent" in under 60 seconds. Google takes you to 2–3 hours of manual work.
How does LinkedIn Auto-Pilot work? Do you need my password?+
No. We never ask for or store your LinkedIn password. You connect via your LinkedIn session cookie (li_at) — the same secure method used by Phantombuster, Expandi, and thousands of sales professionals daily. FreightSignal sends only messages you approve, at daily limits you control (default: 15 connections/day, well within LinkedIn's safe thresholds).
Can I set my home city first, then add international lanes?+
That's exactly how it's designed. Step one when you sign up is setting your home city or state. From there you add focus regions — Mexico, Central America, Caribbean, Asian manufacturing hubs — layer by layer. Solo plan: 1 home market + 2 regions. Growth plan: unlimited regions. Change any time.
What's covered in Central America and the Caribbean?+

Caribbean: Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Trinidad, Barbados, Bahamas, Curaçao, Haiti, Cuba. FreightSignal monitors Spanish-language local publications that Google Alerts routinely misses.

Yes. All plans support CSV export. Growth and Team plans include formatted Excel export with signal data, cargo intelligence, HS codes, and estimated pipeline value — import directly into any CRM. Direct Salesforce connector on the roadmap for Q4 2026.
